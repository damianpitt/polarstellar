# Participation policy

PolarStellar is a personal open-source project maintained by Damiano Pittau, who directs
and reviews its development with AI-assisted tooling.

External code contributions and collaborator applications are not currently being sought.
Please do not open unsolicited pull requests; they may be closed without review.
There is no commitment to implement feature requests, offer support, or review patches.

You are welcome to read the source, use the software, and maintain an independent fork
under the repository license. This participation policy governs the upstream development
workflow; it does not restrict the rights granted by that license.

The maintainer controls the roadmap, releases, architecture, and acceptance of changes.
